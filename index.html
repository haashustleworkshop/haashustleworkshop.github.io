Privacy Policy - HaasHustleWorkshop Pinterest App

Last updated September 2026

This app connects the HaasHustleWorkshop Pinterest account to the Pinterest API so that pins can be created and managed on that single account, in order to promote products sold in the HaasHustleWorkshop Etsy shop.

What this app does

  • Creates and manages Pins and Boards on the HaasHustleWorkshop Pinterest account only.
  • Reads basic account and analytics information for that same account to help improve future posts.

What this app does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to any other Pinterest user.
  • It does not run ads, track visitors, or use cookies of its own.
  • It is not used by the public - it is a single-account tool operated directly by the HaasHustleWorkshop owner.

Data handling

Any data accessed through the Pinterest API is used solely to operate and improve this one account's Pinterest presence. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose beyond that.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if how the app is used changes. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Questions about this app or this policy can be sent to the HaasHustleWorkshop shop owner through the HaasHustleWorkshop Etsy shop.
